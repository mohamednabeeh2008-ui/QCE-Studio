# Whole-Quran Candidate Materialization Contract v0.1

Status: Executable
Campaign: STEP 28-prep

## Purpose

Materialize a Quran-wide candidate manifest from the three frozen harvest channels without converting retrieval into parable identity.

## Scope discipline

This campaign may create QPC candidates. It may not create QPU records, canonical groups, networks, journeys, stations, or formation claims.

The pilot-proven ten-record baseline remains preserved and must be reconciled into the Quran-wide manifest rather than duplicated.

## Required channels

### A — Lexical materialization

Materialize every lexical occurrence in the frozen lexical universe, then classify the occurrence for candidate intake. Root occurrence is never equivalent to parable identity.

Allowed lexical intake outcomes:

- `CANDIDATE_SIGNAL`
- `NON_PARABLE_LEXICAL_USE`
- `AMBIGUOUS_LEXICAL_USE`
- `CROSS_REFERENCE_ONLY`

### B — Structural materialization

Retrieve candidate units through the frozen indicators:

- domain transfer
- image–meaning relation
- comparative architecture
- constructed scenario
- evaluative disclosure
- analogy-like function

Every system-retrieved unit begins as `SYSTEM_DETECTED_CANDIDATE` and requires human review.

### C — Traditional / scholarly materialization

Record units attested in recognized tafsir, balagha, and Quranic-parable literature. Traditional attestation creates a provenance edge, not automatic confirmation.

## Materialization pipeline

1. Ingest channel records.
2. Normalize Quran references.
3. Preserve channel-specific provenance.
4. Detect exact duplicate references.
5. Detect overlapping and nested scopes.
6. Preserve competing boundaries.
7. Reconcile pilot baseline identities.
8. Assign stable QPC IDs only after reconciliation.
9. Run manifest integrity validation.
10. Freeze the Quran-wide candidate manifest version.

## Identity rule

One textual candidate may have many provenance edges.

`ONE CANDIDATE + MANY DISCOVERY ROUTES`

A multi-channel hit must not create multiple QPC records.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No predetermined parable count.
- No formula hit becomes `CONFIRMED` automatically.
- No traditional label becomes canonical identity automatically.
- No structural score becomes a parable decision.
- No overlapping units are merged before boundary review.
- No unresolved unit is forced into binary classification.

## Completion gate

STEP 28-prep is complete only when the manifest contains materialized rows from all three channels, provenance reconciliation has run, integrity validation passes, and the freeze manifest explicitly states its evidence coverage and known gaps.
