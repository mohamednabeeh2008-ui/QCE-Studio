# QPFS Engine Contracts v1.0

Status: Frozen

## Core pipeline

### QPDE — Detection
Question: Why may this unit enter the candidate registry?
May output: detection basis, trigger, harvest channel.
May not output: confirmed parable identity.

### QPBE — Boundary
Question: What are the candidate unit boundaries?
May output: core image, immediate context, full unit, boundary status and rationale.
May not output: interpretive meaning beyond what is necessary to delimit the unit.

### QUP — Unit Profile
Question: What is the unit's structural and discourse profile?
May output: multi-axial structural profile.
May not output: motion proof.

### QPME — Motion Decision
Question: Did meaningful transition occur?
May output: motion decision, state A, transition, state B.
May not output: stations, formation, reader operation.

### QPFSE — Formation Decision
Question: Where, if anywhere, does formation occur?
May output: formation locus and evidence.
May not output: invented reader psychology.

### QPSE — Journey Segmentation
Question: If eligible, what justified journey segments exist?
May output: stations, phases, transitions, turning points, disclosure points.
May not output: formation declaration.

### QROA — Reader Operation Analysis
Question: What operation does the parable perform on reception?
May output: revaluation, disclosure, warning, contrast, recognition, or unresolved.
May not output: image analysis changes or unsupported psychological states.

### QPCM — Correspondence Mapping
Question: What image-to-meaning correspondences are justified?
May output: explicit, contextual, tafsir-supported, or analytical-hypothesis mappings.
May not output: symbols without evidence.

### QPVE — Validation
Question: Is the record structurally, logically, evidentially, and canonically ready?
Internal pipeline: QSV → QLC → QEV → QCR → QDEG.

## Relational layer

### QPSCE — Surah Context
Analyzes before, parable intervention, after, and surah role without merging surah context into unit boundaries.

### QPNL — Unit and Network Relations
Creates proposed links by explicit relation basis. It cannot merge units or canonize networks.

## Discovery layer

### QPDX — Higher-Order Discovery
Status: Locked.
May operate only after the legal corpus campaign. All outputs begin as system hypotheses and cannot canonize themselves.

## No Engine Trespass Rule

- Detection cannot classify.
- Classification cannot prove motion.
- Motion cannot create stations.
- Journey cannot prove formation.
- Formation cannot invent reader psychology.
- Reader operation cannot alter image analysis.
- Correspondence cannot symbolize details without evidence.
- Relations cannot merge units.
- Discovery cannot canonize discoveries.
