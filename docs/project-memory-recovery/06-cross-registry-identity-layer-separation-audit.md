## Purpose

Prevent identity collapse across five historically entangled layers:

CANDIDATE UNIT

This audit creates the Layer Identity Contract. It does not reconstruct missing rows and does not perform new Quranic analysis.

## Core finding

The five layers are not competing names for one object. They answer different questions, use different units of identity, and may have many-to-many mappings.

## LIC-01 — Formation Movement

Definition: a recovered or observed transformation-bearing movement associated with a subject, event, or textual witness.

Identity key: movement identity, not chapter, journey, or product position.

Function: encyclopedia/research description of formative motion.

Count rule: count only when movement identity criteria are satisfied in the recovered source or later canonical specification.

Forbidden equations:

FORMATION MOVEMENT != CANDIDATE UNIT
FORMATION MOVEMENT != NATURAL JOURNEY
FORMATION MOVEMENT != FORMATION STATION
FORMATION MOVEMENT != BOOK CHAPTER

Recovery status: historical asset class; exact inventory pending recovery.

## LIC-02 — Candidate Unit

Definition: a unit admitted to the corpus candidate inventory during Quran-wide scanning.

Identity key: CID or recovered equivalent, with source provenance.

Function: research corpus inventory and trace input.

Count rule: corpus count. Historical checkpoints include 604 and later 632.

Forbidden equations:

CANDIDATE UNIT != FORMATION MOVEMENT
CANDIDATE UNIT != NATURAL JOURNEY
CANDIDATE UNIT != FORMATION STATION
CANDIDATE UNIT != BOOK CHAPTER

A Candidate Unit may later merge with, split across, support, or fail to resolve into journey structures. No one-to-one assumption is allowed.

## LIC-03 — Natural Journey

Definition: an independently resolved formation trajectory after subject, boundary, evidence, and continuity tests.

Identity key: resolved journey identity, not event popularity or character presence.

Function: analytical knowledge layer.

Count rule: only after subject lock and journey identity resolution.

Forbidden equations:

NATURAL JOURNEY != CANDIDATE UNIT
NATURAL JOURNEY != FORMATION STATION
NATURAL JOURNEY != BOOK CHAPTER

Journey count before subject lock is prohibited.

## LIC-04 — Formation Station

Definition: a product-architecture unit selected and shaped for the reader-facing formation sequence.

Identity key: station identity inside the product architecture.

Function: pedagogical/product composition.

Current known count: 250.

Forbidden equations:

FORMATION STATION != NATURAL JOURNEY
FORMATION STATION != CANDIDATE UNIT
FORMATION STATION != BOOK CHAPTER

A station may draw from one journey, part of a journey, multiple related assets, or a later recovered mapping. Exact mapping must be evidenced.

## LIC-05 — Book Chapter

Definition: an editorial container in the product architecture.

Identity key: chapter position and editorial scope.

Function: publication organization.

Current known count: 125 across 3 books.

Forbidden equations:

BOOK CHAPTER != FORMATION STATION
BOOK CHAPTER != NATURAL JOURNEY
BOOK CHAPTER != CANDIDATE UNIT

Known arithmetic: 250 stations / 125 chapters does not prove a universal rule of exactly two stations per chapter.

## Layer Identity Contract

Every record must declare:

- layer_type
- native_id
- source_registry
- evidence_status
- historical_status
- current_status
- canonical_status
- upstream_links
- downstream_links
- mapping_relation
- mapping_confidence

No record may inherit the identity, count, or status of a linked record from another layer.

A mapping edge requires source, relation type, and confidence/evidence status.

## Cardinality policy

The default cardinality across layers is UNKNOWN, not 1:1.

Permitted after evidence:

1:1
1:N
N:1
N:N

Therefore:

632 Candidate Units do not predict the Natural Journey count.
Natural Journey count does not predict 250 Formation Stations.
250 Formation Stations do not prove 125 fixed two-station chapters.

## Cross-registry authority

Historical Asset Registry answers: what existed?
Canonical Knowledge Registry answers: what is accepted now?
Decision Lineage Registry answers: how did status and meaning change?

No registry may silently overwrite another registry's function.
