# PASS-J10 — Composite & Relational Subject Protocol

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Resolve journeys involving more than one salient participant without forcing them into one subject or multiplying journeys mechanically.

## Subject taxonomy

```text
INDIVIDUAL SUBJECT
COLLECTIVE SUBJECT
RELATIONAL SUBJECT
COMPOSITE MISSION SUBJECT
PARALLEL SUBJECTS
PRESERVED SUBJECT
EVENT-CENTRAL NON-FORMATION SUBJECT
```

## Decision sequence

```text
LIST PARTICIPANTS
→ IDENTIFY ACTION/RESPONSE CHAINS
→ TEST SHARED T0
→ TEST SHARED CHALLENGE
→ TEST SHARED TRANSITION
→ TEST SHARED T1
→ ASSIGN SUBJECT TYPE
→ COUNT JOURNEYS
```

## Governing rules

1. Shared event does not imply shared journey.
2. Shared trial does not imply shared transition.
3. Cooperation does not automatically create a composite subject.
4. A composite subject requires a mission-level action unit that cannot be represented accurately as independent parallel trajectories.
5. A relational subject requires the relationship itself to be the formation-bearing unit.
6. Parallel subjects preserve separate journeys even when their actions are synchronized.
7. A preserved subject may be central to the event without being the formation agent.

## Retrospective tests

### Ibrahim and the son — EF-IBR-07

Decision: PARALLEL SUBJECTS, not automatic composite journey.

Reason:
- shared trial: yes
- identical reception: no
- identical response chain: no
- automatic merge: prohibited

Result: father arc and son arc remain separately testable.

### Moses and Aaron — mission context

Decision: COMPOSITE MISSION SUBJECT is allowed as a hypothesis only where the mission action is explicitly joint. It does not erase individual arcs.

### Mother of Moses / infant Moses / sister

Decision:
- mother: responding formation subject
- sister: tracking/action subject
- infant Moses: preserved subject

Result: character centrality does not control journey ownership.

### Magicians and Moses

Decision:
- magicians: collective formation subject for their conversion trajectory
- Moses: event-central, not primary formation subject of that trajectory

### Ibrahim and his people

Decision:
- Ibrahim: primary subject arcs
- people: independent counter-journey candidate
