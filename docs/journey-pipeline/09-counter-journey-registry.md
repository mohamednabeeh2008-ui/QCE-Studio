# PASS-J09 — Counter-Journey Registry

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Represent resistant, reversing, collapsing, or opposition trajectories without contaminating the primary journey count.

## Core rule

`COUNTER-JOURNEY != NEGATIVE LABEL`

A counter-journey requires a traceable subject trajectory. Mere opposition, wrongdoing, or antagonist presence is insufficient.

## Admission gates

```text
CJ1 subject resolved
CJ2 T0 resolved
CJ3 transition sequence present
CJ4 T1 or closure present
CJ5 trajectory is independent from primary subject
CJ6 evidence level separated
CJ7 duplicate with primary journey prohibited
```

## Registry states

- CANDIDATE
- HELD
- PROMOTED
- BLOCKED
- DEPRECATED

## Identifier policy

```yaml
hypothesis_prefix: CJH-
canonical_prefix: CJ-
```

Counter-journeys do not consume `J-ID` identifiers unless a future governance decision explicitly unifies registries. Current policy keeps them distinct.

## Counting policy

```text
PRIMARY JOURNEY COUNT
!=
COUNTER-JOURNEY COUNT
```

Both may later contribute to the wider formation network, but they remain separate analytical populations.
