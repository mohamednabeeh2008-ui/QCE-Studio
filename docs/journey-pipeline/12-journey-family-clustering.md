# PASS-J12 — Journey Family Clustering

Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Group journeys by structural resemblance without merging their canonical identities or allowing popular themes to replace transition structure.

## Cluster basis

A Journey Family is an analytical grouping based on a comparable transition pattern. It is not a Journey, Event Family, theme, moral, or reader lesson.

## Similarity dimensions

```text
trigger_type
agency_type
knowledge_gap
challenge_type
correction_mode
transition_shape
T1_capacity
closure_type
```

## Clustering rules

1. Same capacity does not automatically mean same journey family.
2. Same moral label is insufficient.
3. Different subjects may belong to one family.
4. One journey may have one primary family and optional secondary affinities.
5. Family membership never changes the J-ID.
6. Clusters remain provisional until the inventory is large enough for stability testing.
